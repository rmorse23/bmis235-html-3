NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series

The O'Reilly Auto Parts Series is the second level of NASCAR. It's the last step before the Cup Series, so there are a lot of young drivers trying to show they're ready for Cup. Sometimes Cup drivers race in it too.

History

This series started in 1982. You might know it by one of its old names, like the Busch Series, the Nationwide Series, or the Xfinity Series. In 2025 O'Reilly Auto Parts became the sponsor, so now it's called the O'Reilly Auto Parts Series.

The Car

The cars kind of look like Cup cars, but they're a little lighter and don't have as much horsepower. There are three car brands in this series:

  • Chevrolet (Camaro)
  • Ford (Mustang)
  • Toyota (GR Supra)

The Season

There are 33 races in the season. Most of them happen at the same track as a Cup race, usually the day before. So if you go to a race weekend, you can usually see both series.
