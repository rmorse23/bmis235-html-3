NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series

The Truck Series is the third national series in NASCAR, and honestly it might be the most fun to watch. Instead of cars, the drivers race pickup trucks. The races are shorter, so everybody is really aggressive and there's a lot of bumping.

History

The Truck Series started in 1995, and back then it was called the SuperTruck Series. Craftsman was the first sponsor. Other companies sponsored it for a while, but Craftsman came back in 2023.

The Trucks

The trucks are supposed to look like normal pickups you could buy, but underneath they're full race trucks with V8 engines. These are the brands that race:

  • Chevrolet (Silverado)
  • Ford (F-150)
  • Toyota (Tundra)
  • Ram

The Season

The season has around 25 races. A lot of them are on the same weekend as the other series, but they also go to some tracks that Cup doesn't race at. For a lot of young drivers, this is the first time they get to race on TV.
