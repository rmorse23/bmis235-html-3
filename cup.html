NASCAR Cup Series

The Cup Series is the top level of NASCAR. This is where all the best drivers and the biggest teams race. When somebody says they watch NASCAR, they are probably talking about the Cup Series.

History

The first race was all the way back in 1949. Back then it was called the Strictly Stock division. The name has changed a bunch of times because of sponsors, like Winston Cup, Nextel Cup, Sprint Cup, and Monster Energy Cup. Since 2020 it's just been called the NASCAR Cup Series.

The Car

In 2022 NASCAR switched everyone over to the "Next Gen" car. A lot of the parts on it are the same for every team, which makes the racing closer and saves teams money. There are three car brands in the Cup Series:

  • Chevrolet (Camaro ZL1)
  • Ford (Mustang Dark Horse)
  • Toyota (Camry XSE)

The Season

The season goes from February all the way to November and has 36 races that count for points. It starts with the Daytona 500, which is the biggest race of the year. Drivers get points every race, and at the end of the year the top drivers battle it out for the championship.
