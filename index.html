NASCAR's National Series

A lot of people don't know this, but NASCAR actually has three different national series, not just one. There's the Cup Series, the O'Reilly Auto Parts Series, and the Craftsman Truck Series.

The Cup Series is the big one that most people think of. O'Reilly is the level right under it, and the Truck Series is where most drivers start out. Most drivers move up from Trucks to O'Reilly and then to Cup if they're good enough.
